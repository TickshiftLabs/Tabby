# Problem Statement

Existing tab managers (e.g. OneTab) are excellent at emergency storage
but poor at retrieval and reasoning.

They suffer from:

- flat lists of links
- weak or manual grouping
- no metadata (domains, intent, time)
- poor search
- no understanding of context

As a result, users hesitate to archive tabs and instead keep them open,
leading to overwhelm.

Tabby solves this by turning tabs into structured, searchable data.
