# Vision

Tabs are externalized working memory.

People do not keep many tabs open because they are disorganized.
They do it because tabs represent ideas, tasks, research threads,
and unfinished thinking.

Tabby treats tabs as _mental state_, not browser noise.

Instead of forcing users to close tabs, Tabby:

- captures context
- stores it safely
- makes retrieval easy
- allows letting go without anxiety

The goal is not productivity.
The goal is cognitive relief.
